Vital Water Research

The research program behind NymphCount. Field ecology and freshwater science by Lee Vitaly Wyszynski, Lebanon Valley College — including ongoing field research on benthic macroinvertebrate communities in Pennsylvania streams.

Papers

Selected papers

Stream ecology · Biomonitoring

Biological Integrity vs. Chemical Snapshots: Are Macroinvertebrates the Best Indicator of Aquatic Ecosystem Health?

Weighs benthic macroinvertebrates as living indicators of aquatic ecosystem health against chemical snapshot methods — what the animals remember that a water sample can’t.

Manuscript available on request

Wetland birds · Conservation

Life History and Conservation Planning for the Red-winged Blackbird (Agelaius phoeniceus)

A synthesis of the red-winged blackbird’s life history, reproductive ecology, and behavior, developed into an action-oriented conservation plan for campus wetland habitats.

Manuscript available on request

Invasive species · Freshwater conservation

Friend, foe, or food? The unresolved case of invasive rusty crayfish and the endangered eastern hellbender

Synthesizes the evidence for and against a harmful role for invasive rusty crayfish in the decline of the endangered eastern hellbender — predator, prey, or bystander — and what recovery planning should do while the case is open.

Manuscript available on request
